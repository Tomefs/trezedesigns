Signature Collection rebrand


During my internship at Camera and Suitcase, a marketing agency belonging to the Total Fun Group, I worked on the rebranding of Signature Collection, a premium collection encompassing the Honeymoon, Premium and Wedding Destination segments.

The project aimed to establish a more refined, contemporary and cohesive visual identity, positioning Signature Collection as a seal of quality and luxury experiences. The new identity was inspired by the visual language associated with luxury and Old Money aesthetics, while maintaining a distinctive and versatile system capable of adapting to the individual characteristics of each segment.

My role focused on the development of the visual identity and its supporting graphic system. This included the creation and refinement of the Signature Collection logo system, alongside dedicated identities for Honeymoon, Premium and Wedding Destination. Different logo configurations and applications were developed to ensure flexibility across communication formats while preserving consistency throughout the collection.
